💡 How SurfaceWatch Calculates Risk

SurfaceWatch calculates risk by evaluating both Vulnerability Severity (CVSS) and Graph Topology (Network Position):

1. CVSS Vulnerability Score

CVSS rates software flaws from 0.0 to 10.0 based on exploitation difficulty and impact:

  • Critical (9.0–10.0): Urgent public flaw
  • High (7.0–8.9): Serious working exploit
  • Medium (4.0–6.9): Moderate real-world risk
  • Low (0.1–3.9): Minor exposure
2. Network Topology Position

Even an asset with zero CVEs is dangerous if it acts as a central hub or chokepoint:

  • Chokepoints: Assets sitting between public internet and internal systems.
  • Trust Hubs: Nodes that transfer trust across multiple subdomains or services.
Formula: Combined Score = (CVSS Risk + (Betweenness × 5 + PageRank × 5)) / 2
